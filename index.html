Shuqi Zhao (赵澍淇)

Hi! I am a Ph.D. student (Sep. 2023 - ) at UC Berkeley under the supervision of Prof. Masayoshi Tomizuka in the Mechanical Systems Control (MSC) Lab, which is affiliated with the Berkeley Artificial Intelligence Research (BAIR). Prior to this, I obtained my B.Eng. degree (Sep. 2019 - Jun. 2023) in Control Science and Engineering from Zhejiang University, majoring in automation with an honors minor at Chu Kochen Honors College.

My research interests lie in robot learning, with a particular emphasis on world action models and dexterous manipulation, including learning from human demonstrations, sim-to-real transfer.

I am seeking for reseacher internship opportunities in industry in 2027, please feel free to contact me!
